How It Works

Creator-controlled publishing

The application assists its operator with preparing short-form video content. Finished videos are reviewed before publication.

Supported social media accounts are connected using official authorization methods, subject to platform permissions.

Publishing is designed to require explicit approval by the account owner. The application is intended for the operator's own accounts and is not offered as a public service.
