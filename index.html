Private Gaming Creator Application

Milqka AI Clips

Milqka AI Clips is a private desktop application for transforming gaming recordings and Twitch streams into short-form videos. It assists with selecting highlights, editing vertical clips, adding subtitles, reviewing finished content, and publishing creator-approved videos to authorized social media accounts, including YouTube.

What Is Milqka AI Clips?

Purpose of the Application

Milqka AI Clips is developed for a gaming content creator who wants to simplify the production and publication of highlights from their own gameplay and livestreams.

The application processes creator-provided video recordings, identifies potential highlights, prepares vertical video edits, and supports subtitles and visual effects.

The creator reviews and approves content before it is submitted for publication. The application is designed for private creator use and is not a publicly available video hosting or social networking service.

How Milqka AI Clips Works

  1. The creator provides their own gameplay recording or livestream video.
  2. The application analyzes the recording and prepares potential gaming highlights.
  3. Selected moments are edited into vertical short-form videos with subtitles and optional visual effects.
  4. The creator reviews the resulting videos and approves the content and publication details.
  5. Approved videos can be uploaded to authorized social media accounts through supported official APIs.

Milqka AI Clips is designed to publish only content approved by the account owner.

YouTube API Integration

Why the Application Uses YouTube API

Milqka AI Clips uses the official YouTube Data API to upload approved gaming videos to the creator's own YouTube channel.

The application requests the youtube.upload OAuth permission to upload videos on behalf of the channel owner after authorization.

Google OAuth Authorization

The YouTube channel owner authorizes the application through Google's official OAuth consent process. Authorization is required before the application can access the permitted YouTube upload functionality.

The application does not require visitors to this website to sign in. This website is an informational page about the desktop application.

Creator Control

Videos are prepared for review and must be approved before publication. The application is intended to operate only on accounts authorized by their owner.

Revoking Access

The channel owner may revoke the application's Google access at any time through Google Account Connections .

For more information about data handling, authorization, and deletion requests, please read our Privacy Policy.
