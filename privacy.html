Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Milqka AI Clips accesses, uses, stores, shares, retains and handles information in connection with its video editing and authorized publishing functionality, including the use of Google OAuth and YouTube API Services.

1. About Milqka AI Clips

Milqka AI Clips is a private desktop application designed to help a gaming content creator prepare highlights from gameplay recordings and livestreams, edit vertical short-form videos, add subtitles, review content and publish approved videos to authorized social media accounts.

The application is intended for use by its operator and is not offered as a public service to third-party users. The website provides information about the application and does not require visitors to sign in.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the features and connected accounts used, Milqka AI Clips may process:

  • Account identifiers, channel identifiers and basic account or channel information returned by authorized platform APIs.
  • OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens and related authorization information.
  • Creator-provided gameplay recordings, livestream videos and associated audio.
  • Generated clips, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, captions and subtitles.
  • Publishing preferences, creator approvals, upload identifiers, upload status and publication records.
  • Technical diagnostic information, including API responses, error codes and timestamps needed to troubleshoot publishing operations.

The application does not ask the operator to provide their Google or social media account passwords. Supported account connections use the relevant platform's authorization process.

3. Google OAuth and YouTube API Services

Milqka AI Clips uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API, to support uploading creator-approved videos to a YouTube channel authorized by its owner.

Google OAuth Permissions

The application requests the youtube.upload OAuth scope to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel on behalf of its owner.

During account authorization and operation, the application may retrieve the authorized YouTube channel identifier to verify that uploads are directed to the intended channel.

How YouTube Information Is Used

Information obtained through YouTube API Services is used for the following purposes:

  • Confirm that the connected YouTube channel matches the channel selected by the operator.
  • Authenticate authorized YouTube API requests.
  • Upload creator-approved videos and accompanying publication metadata.
  • Retrieve or reconcile upload and video processing status.
  • Identify technical errors and prevent unintended duplicate uploads.

YouTube authorization is used for the application's video publishing workflow. The application does not request access to unrelated Google services as part of this integration.

YouTube Terms and Google Privacy Policy

By using the application's YouTube integration, the operator is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

Google's processing of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy .

The application uses YouTube API Services subject to applicable YouTube API Services Terms of Service .

4. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Connect and authenticate authorized accounts.
  • Analyze creator-provided gaming recordings.
  • Prepare and edit short-form gaming videos.
  • Generate subtitles and publishing metadata.
  • Support creator review and approval.
  • Upload approved content to connected accounts.
  • Monitor publication and processing status.
  • Investigate errors and maintain application reliability.

Milqka AI Clips is designed to require creator approval before initiating publication.

5. Local Storage and Security

Gaming recordings, generated videos, editing metadata and publication records are primarily processed and stored on the operator's local computer.

The application maintains authorization information and technical publishing state needed to operate connected integrations and resume or reconcile upload operations.

Access to the operator's computer and application credentials should be restricted to authorized persons. OAuth tokens and other credentials must not be published in public source code repositories.

Necessary information is transmitted to connected platform APIs when the operator uses authorized publishing functionality.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Milqka AI Clips does not sell personal information.

When authorized publishing is performed, the application transmits the information necessary for the requested operation to the selected platform.

For YouTube uploads, this may include video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy settings and authorization information required by the YouTube API.

Other supported integrations may involve TikTok, Instagram, Threads or their respective official APIs, depending on which accounts the operator connects and uses.

These platforms process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Information obtained from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

7. Data Retention

Local video files, editing metadata, approval records and publication history may be retained for as long as needed for the operator's video production and publishing workflow.

OAuth credentials and associated integration state may be retained while an account remains connected and the authorization is needed for permitted operations.

The operator may remove local files and records that are no longer needed, subject to any applicable legal or operational requirements.

8. Revoking Google Access

The operator may review and revoke Milqka AI Clips access to their Google account at any time using:

Google Account Connections .

Revoking Google authorization prevents further API operations that require that authorization, unless access is granted again.

Revoking access does not automatically remove videos that were previously published to YouTube or delete application files stored locally.
