Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Milqka AI Clips is a private, desktop-based application designed to process gaming stream recordings, create short-form videos, and assist the application operator in publishing content to their own social media accounts.

The application is intended for personal use by its operator and is not offered as a public service for third-party users.

2. Information We Process

When a social media account is connected through an authorized integration, Milqka AI Clips may process:

  • Social media account identifiers and basic profile information.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens.
  • Video files, thumbnails, captions, and publishing preferences.
  • Upload status, timestamps, and API error information.
  • Local video editing metadata and approval records.

The application does not require social media account passwords. Authentication is performed through the relevant platform's official authorization process.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only as necessary to:

  • Connect and authenticate the operator's accounts.
  • Prepare and review gaming highlight videos.
  • Upload videos approved by the operator.
  • Apply captions and available publishing settings.
  • Monitor publication status and diagnose technical errors.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the integration.

Milqka AI Clips is designed to require explicit operator approval before initiating publication.

4. TikTok Integration

TikTok account access is requested through TikTok's official OAuth authorization process.

Subject to the permissions granted and the availability of TikTok's APIs, the application may use authorized account information and publishing permissions to upload approved videos.

TikTok may independently process information according to its own privacy policy: TikTok Privacy Policy .

5. Storage and Security

Gaming recordings, generated videos, and editing metadata are primarily processed and stored on the operator's local computer.

Authentication credentials and tokens must be protected against unauthorized access and must not be committed to public source code repositories.

Authorized video uploads and authentication requests may transmit necessary information to the connected social media platform.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

Milqka AI Clips does not sell personal information.

Information is transmitted to TikTok or another connected platform only as necessary to perform authorized account operations and approved content publication.

The application may use local software components for video processing and analysis.

7. Data Retention

Local video files, editing metadata, and publication records may be retained for as long as they are needed for the operator's editing workflow.

Authentication tokens are retained only while required for the authorized integration and should be removed when the account is disconnected.

Data no longer required for the application may be deleted by the operator, subject to applicable legal obligations.

8. Account Disconnection and Data Deletion

The operator may revoke TikTok access through the account's authorized application settings.

Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of information associated with Milqka AI Clips can be sent to: mil8d1ka@gmail.com .

Revoking access prevents further authorized API operations, but does not automatically delete videos already published on TikTok.

9. Children's Privacy

Milqka AI Clips is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, its integrations, or applicable legal requirements.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
